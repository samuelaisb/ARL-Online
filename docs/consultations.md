# Expertise consultations

The reference for mentors, consultation requests, scheduling, Zoom, follow-ups, item owners and their emails. The project map is `AGENTS.md`; the short invariants agents load automatically are in `.cursor/rules/expertise-consultations.mdc`. Moved here from `AGENTS.md` on 2026-11-05. Keep it accurate when consultation behavior changes.

Use this doc when changing the Expertise category, consultation request form, expert scheduling, cancellation, Zoom meetings, related emails/Slack payloads, or migrations `005_expertise.sql` / `006_consultation_scheduling.sql` / `007_expertise_copy.sql` / `008_consultation_follow_up.sql` / `015_item_owners_and_reminders.sql` (owners, reminders). Consultations **reuse** the reservations table and `POST /api/inventory/:id/reservations` — they are not a separate booking system.

**Lifecycle:** `pending` (member submitted) → `reserved` (expert or admin set the meeting time; Zoom meeting created) → `cancelled` (member, expert, or admin). Admins can also `refuse` a pending request. A scheduled meeting that hasn't started can be **moved** to a new time (`meeting_at` changes, the row stays `reserved`, the Zoom meeting moves with it). A **follow-up** is a new row the expert books from the day of a scheduled meeting (its Montréal date is today or earlier), starting after it; it is written straight as `reserved` (Zoom meeting created) with `follow_up_of` pointing at that meeting. Staff can also book a consultation for anyone from `/admin` → **Create Reservation**: a new row written straight as `reserved` with its Zoom meeting and `booked_by_staff` (see **Admin flow** step 8). There is no `completed` status: a `reserved` row whose meeting ended (`meetingAt` + 60 min) is **held**, and the server and client both derive that with `isConsultationHeld()` in `src/lib/reservation-rules.js`. A held meeting can't be cancelled, and admin deletes treat it as a record only (no Zoom delete, no emails).

**Who is the expert:** the signed-in user whose **confirmed** Supabase email matches the item's `expert_email` (case-insensitive, compared in JS). Experts use the normal `/account` page — no separate role or table. Items without `expert_email` are scheduled by their owner (below) or by admins. Because everything is tied to the address, an address change moves things: staff giving a mentor a new address in **Mentors** hands their open consultations to it (**Admin flow** step 2), and an account's own email change moves its mentor item and reservations with it (migration 014).

**Who hosts it (the effective owner):** `itemOwnerEmail(item)` in `inventory-store.js`: the mentor's `expert_email` when set, else the item's `owner_email` (migration 015; the admin who added the mentor, the member who published it with **Share Expertise**, or samuel@apathyisboring.com for items from before 015). `isItemOwner(item, email)` compares it like `emailsMatch`. A **confirmed** account that is the effective owner but not the expert (a mentor with no address of its own) is `isOwner` in `loadConsultationForUser`: its consultations show on its `/account` agenda as a mentor's do (`listConsultationsForUser` puts them in `asExpert`), and it can do everything the expert does (schedule, cancel, message the member, choose a new time, book a follow-up). Emails word what it does by `consultationHostVoice()` in `server.js`: staff (an admin account) speak as the library team (`messageFrom` / `cancelledBy` `'admin'`), anyone else as the mentor (`'expert'`; only a member whose own profile lost its expert email). The schedule, move and follow-up log lines and the follow-up's Slack sentence name the role as `expert`, `owner` or `admin` (`consultationHostRole()`); the cancel log line names `cancelled_by`. Such a mentor's emails still go to its `expert_email` only, so with none set only the member is emailed.

## Member flow

1. Filter **Expertise** (`/expertise`). The list uses the same cards and grid as the other categories (3 cards per row from 900px, one column on small screens). Each card shows the photo in the standard image frame, the name, and the **short text**, with no availability badge. **Request Consultation** opens `/{tag}/{slug}?reserve=1`.
2. Opening an expert shows the **long text** plus `ConsultationRequestForm.svelte` in a narrow overlay (no availability badge, no three-column calendar layout).
3. Member gives up to three times (**Date** / **From** / **To** rows) and/or their availability in their own words, plus a **summary** (auth-gated like Reserve). The client joins them into the one `timeSlots` string (a line per time in the member's language and device time zone, e.g. "Tue, Nov 10, 2:00 – 4:00 p.m. EST", then their own words; max 500) and posts `{ timeSlots, summary }`.
4. Server stores a `pending` reservation with `start_date`/`end_date` = submission date (`toDateKey(new Date())`), plus `time_slots` / `request_summary`. **No date collision check** (multiple members may request the same expert). A member can have only **one open request per expert**: if they already have a pending or upcoming (not yet held) consultation on that item, `addReservation` returns 409 `consultation_already_open` inside the item lock. Each account can also create at most **10 reservations or consultation requests per 24 hours** (429 `daily_request_limit`). Both errors show in EN/FR in the form (and the 429 in the calendar). So do the route's other two 429s, which carry codes as well: the 5-pending cap (`pending_request_limit`) and the IP limiter (`reservation_rate_limited`).
5. Slack webhook fires. Member gets “request received” email; **expert gets “new consultation request” email** linking to `/account`. Kimchi shows `kimchi.consultation_sent`, and the form gives way to a **Request sent** panel (what was sent, **Go to your account**). The form says, in its intro and in a **Who sees this** strip right above **Send request**, that the request and the member's account email go to the named expert and to the Apathy is Boring team (`consultation.intro`, `consultation.sharing_note`).
6. `/account` → the agenda (`AccountAgenda.svelte`; see `docs/frontend.md` → **Components**). A pending request sits under **Coming up** → **Sent, waiting for a reply**: its sticker, the request date, the expert's email, **Show request details** (their times and summary) and a Lavender strip saying the expert will pick a time. Once scheduled it moves onto the timeline on its Montréal day: the time, the expert's email, the passcode, the summary, and **Join Zoom** as its one visible button. Cancelled, declined, and finished meetings (60 minutes after the start) go to the collapsed **Earlier** section. A follow-up the expert booked shows a **Follow-up** sticker (`account_consultations.follow_up_badge`) and the expert's optional note under **Follow-up note**; the member can cancel it like any scheduled meeting. Members can't book follow-ups themselves; they can request a new consultation once nothing is open with that expert. **Withdraw request** (pending) or **Cancel meeting** (scheduled) is in the entry's **More** menu and asks an inline question first (`account_agenda.withdraw_request_confirm` / `account_consultations.cancel_confirm_member`). After it, Kimchi says `kimchi.consultation_withdrawn` (pending) or `consultation_cancelled_member` (scheduled), "{title} has been told", only when the response has `otherPartyEmailed: true` (a mentor may have no email); otherwise the `_plain` version. On load Kimchi says at most one thing about the lists, in the order meeting starting soon (B6) > closed by the other side (B4) > new requests for an expert (B5) > a request waiting a week or more (C8, with a **Contact us** link to `/about#contact`); see `docs/automated-notifications.md`. A scheduled meeting shows a **Starts in N min** sticker (Lemon) from 15 minutes before its start and **Happening now** (Grape) until it ends, and rows the other side cancelled or declined since this browser's last visit show for that visit under **Changed since your last visit**, outside **Earlier**.
7. **Message the mentor** and **Suggest a new time** are in the entry's **More** menu: messages on a pending request or a scheduled meeting not yet held, a new time only while the request is pending or the meeting hasn't started. Each opens one `ReservationMessageForm` panel in the card (one panel open at a time on the whole agenda) whose text (max 2000, `RESERVATION_MESSAGE_MAX`) goes to `POST /api/reservations/:reservationId/message` with `as: 'member'` and `kind` `message` or `new_time`. The panel shows the booking's conversation above the field (`GET /api/reservations/:reservationId/messages?as=member`, which marks it read). The server saves the message (migration 016) and emails the effective owner (the mentor's `expert_email`, else `owner_email`, else the library inbox `CONTACT_TO`) only when they've opened the conversation since the last such email; the email's Reply-To is the library's and it points to `/account` (`docs/automated-emails.md` trigger 13). The meeting doesn't move until the mentor picks a time. After a send the message joins the conversation, the panel stays open with focus back in the field, the status line says `reservation_message.sent_status` / `suggested_status`, and Kimchi `kimchi.message_sent` / `time_suggestion_sent`. Unread messages from the mentor show as an **N new messages** sticker on the entry (`unreadMessages` in `GET /api/account/consultations`) that opens the conversation. The day before a scheduled meeting the member and the mentor get a reminder email (trigger 12).

## Share Expertise

Every signed-in account can open **Share Expertise** from a button on the account panel (`AccountShareExpertise.svelte`). The form stays closed until that button is clicked, then appears above the agenda. Closing it keeps any unsaved edits.

1. A **confirmed** email is required. Unconfirmed accounts see a prompt to confirm before the form is shown (`GET /api/account/mentor-profile` returns `{ profile: null, emailConfirmed: false }`).
2. The member publishes **one** mentor profile: name (max 120), photo, short text, and long text. `POST /api/account/mentor-profile` creates an expertise item and sets `expert_email` to the JWT email, and `owner_email` too (015), so the profile stays the account's to manage if staff later clear or change the mentor's address. The client cannot choose a different address. A second create returns **409** with the existing profile.
3. The profile is a normal expertise item, so it appears on the Expertise list immediately. The slug is generated once and stays fixed when the profile is edited.
4. **Edit** is available when that item's `expert_email` matches the account email (case-insensitive). `PATCH /api/account/mentor-profile` updates name, short text, long text, and an optional new photo. It does not change `expert_email` or the slug. If several expertise items share the email, the earliest one is the profile they edit, and create stays blocked. An admin-created mentor with that email opens in the same editor.
5. Photos are capped at 2,000,000 data-URL characters (413 `image_too_large`) and must be an uploaded JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF data URL (400 `image_invalid`; unlike the admin routes, `/assets/inventory/` seed paths are refused); the client shows both in EN/FR. The routes use a **3mb** JSON limit. The profile `image` comes back as a `/media/items/...` URL, and sending that URL back on `PATCH` means "photo unchanged". Creates and updates are rate-limited (10 per 15 minutes) and serialized with an in-process per-email lock (single Cloud Run instance). Kimchi: `kimchi.mentor_profile_published` / `kimchi.mentor_profile_updated`.
6. Profiles go live without review, so each successful publish, and each edit that changes something, posts a **Slack staff alert** (`notifySlackAlert()`, status `mentor_profile_published` / `mentor_profile_updated`; `request_summary` names the profile, the account email, a rename and the changed fields, and links the page). The route waits for it (at most 5 s) and a Slack failure never fails the save. No email is sent.

## Expert flow

1. Logs in (or registers) with the `expert_email` address and opens `/account`. Each pending request waits at the top of the agenda under **Needs you** (with a count sticker), as a card titled **Pick a time for {email}**; scheduled meetings sit on the **Coming up** timeline, and cancelled, declined and finished meetings go to the collapsed **Earlier** section. The effective owner of a mentor with no address of its own (**Who hosts it** above) gets the same entries and every step below.
2. Each **Needs you** card has a Lemon band with the title, the request date, a **Follow-up** sticker when it is one and the item link; below it, on the left, the member's times and summary in boxes that keep their line breaks (one column on phones), and on the right a **Meeting date and time** field: a visible native `datetime-local` input (`MeetingTimePicker.svelte`). Tapping it opens the system date-time picker on iOS and Android; desktop browsers show editable date and time segments. The value is read as local time by `parseMeetingTimeInput()` and sent as ISO. Then **Schedule meeting** (its one visible button, with **More** beside it). No time shows `meeting_time_required`; a time in the past shows `meeting_time_past` (iOS doesn't enforce `min` in its picker). Both appear under the field, which gets focus and `aria-invalid`. A valid time asks an inline question that names it (`account_consultations.schedule_confirm`); Yes books exactly that time. Under it, an optional **Message to the member** (`ConfirmationMessage.svelte`, max 1000, `CONFIRMATION_MESSAGE_MAX`; hidden when the row has no member email) goes into both scheduled emails and is not stored; the field clears after a successful schedule.
3. Schedule → `POST /api/consultations/:reservationId/schedule { meetingAt, message }` → Zoom meeting created → `reserved` with `meeting_at` + `zoom_*` → both get “scheduled” emails with Zoom link and `.ics` invite. Kimchi `kimchi.consultation_scheduled` (or `kimchi.consultation_scheduled_plain`, without "invitations are on their way", when the response has `emailSent === false`); a hidden status line announces it and focus moves to the meeting's title on the timeline.
4. **Decline request** (pending) or **Cancel meeting** (scheduled), from the entry's **More** menu, after an inline question (`account_agenda.decline_request_confirm`, or `_no_email` when the member's account was deleted; `account_consultations.cancel_confirm_expert`) → `POST /api/consultations/:reservationId/cancel` → `cancelled` + `cancelled_by: 'expert'` (an owner: `consultationHostVoice`, so `'admin'` for staff) → Zoom meeting deleted → both emailed (a declined request: "Consultation request declined", the route's `declined` option). A declined request (`isDeclinedRequest`: `cancelled` by `expert` or `admin` with no `meetingAt`) reads **Declined** with **Declined by the expert** / **the library team** (`account_agenda.declined_by_*`) on both sides, and the member's B4 bubble is `kimchi.consultation_declined`. Kimchi `kimchi.consultation_closed_expert` (pending; "Request declined") / `consultation_cancelled_expert` (scheduled), or the `_plain` version when the response says the email didn't go out (`emailSent` or `otherPartyEmailed` false); a hidden status line announces it and focus moves to the **Earlier** toggle. A held meeting returns 400 `consultation_already_held` (`account_consultations.already_held`); the UI already hides **Cancel meeting** once a meeting is finished.
5. **Book a follow-up** (agreed during the call): from the day of a `reserved` meeting (its Montréal date is today or earlier, so from the morning of the meeting), **Book a follow-up** (`account_consultations.follow_up_open`, in **More**) opens a panel in the card with a `MeetingTimePicker` and an optional note (max 1000, `FOLLOW_UP_NOTE_MAX` in `src/lib/expertise-fields.js`). **Book follow-up** → inline question (`account_consultations.follow_up_confirm`) → `POST /api/consultations/:reservationId/follow-up { meetingAt, note }` → `bookFollowUpConsultation` (item lock) creates the Zoom meeting and inserts a new `reserved` row for the same member (`follow_up_of` = the source id, `request_summary` = the note, no `time_slots`, `start_date` = today in Montréal) → both get the scheduled emails worded as a follow-up, and staff get a Slack alert. The new row is added to the list, and Kimchi says `kimchi.follow_up_booked` (`_plain` when `emailSent === false`); focus moves to the new meeting's title. The button shows only on the member's latest meeting with that expert whose day has come and only while nothing else is open between them (no pending request, no upcoming meeting; `canBookFollowUp()` in `src/lib/reservation-rules.js`, shared with `/admin`), and never on a meeting whose member deleted their account (no member email; the server refuses it too), so it is on today's meeting on the timeline (before and during the call) and on a meeting that just ended under **Earlier**. Same time checks as scheduling (`meeting_time_required` / `meeting_time_past`), and the follow-up must start after the meeting it follows (400 `follow_up_before_meeting`, `account_consultations.follow_up_before_meeting`). Its errors show inside its panel.
6. **Message the member** (in **More**): on an entry with a member email that is pending or scheduled (held meetings too), a `ReservationMessageForm` panel (`account_consultations.message_label_member`) shows the conversation and posts to `POST /api/reservations/:reservationId/message` with `as: 'owner'`. The message is saved (016) and the member is emailed when they've read the conversation since the last email, with the library's Reply-To (never the mentor's or owner's address); it is signed with the mentor's name, or "The Activist Resource Library team" when staff who aren't the mentor send it (shown as **The library team** in the conversation, `byStaff`). Kimchi `kimchi.message_sent`. The member's unread messages show as an **N new messages** sticker on the entry.
7. **Choose a new time** (in **More**): on a scheduled meeting that hasn't started, a panel with `MeetingTimePicker` (starts at the current time; `account_consultations.reschedule_intro`), an optional **Message to the member** (`ConfirmationMessage`) and **Move meeting**. The client refuses an empty or past time and the meeting's current minute (`reschedule_unchanged`), then asks an inline question (`account_consultations.reschedule_confirm`) and on Yes posts `POST /api/consultations/:reservationId/reschedule { meetingAt, message }`. The server moves the Zoom meeting in the item lock (**Zoom** → Move) and saves `meeting_at`, then emails both people "Consultation moved" with the new and old times and an updated `.ics` (`docs/automated-emails.md` trigger 15). A move that would put a follow-up at or before the meeting it follows, either way, is 400 `follow_up_before_meeting`. On success the entry takes the new time (moving to that day on the timeline), focus goes to its title, the status line says `rescheduled_status` (+ `rescheduled_emailed_status` unless `emailSent === false`) and Kimchi `kimchi.consultation_rescheduled` (`_plain` when `emailSent === false`). Errors show inside the panel.
8. The entry's **More** menu holds **Choose a new time**, **Book a follow-up**, **Message the member** and **Decline request** / **Cancel meeting** (the ones the entry allows, in that order); one panel and one question are open at a time on the whole agenda (`openPanel`, `confirmState` in `AccountAgenda`), and opening one closes the others. The day before a scheduled meeting the mentor gets a reminder too (trigger 12).

## Admin flow

1. Add expertise items via Add Item → **Expertise**; optional **Expert email** (`expertEmail` → `inventory_items.expert_email`; admin-only, stripped from public API), required **Short text** (`body`, max 180, shown on the list) and **Long text** (`longBody` → `inventory_items.long_body`, max 4000, shown when the expert is opened). Apply `007_expertise_copy.sql` before saving long text. The admin who adds the item becomes its owner (`owner_email`, 015), so a mentor added with no expert email lists its consultations on that admin's `/account` (**Who hosts it**). Items saved before separate fields exist keep working: the first paragraph of `body` is the list text, and the rest (after a blank line) is the overlay text, until `longBody` is set.
2. **Mentors** lists expertise items (photo, name, expert email, short text). **Edit** saves name, expert email (blank clears it), short text, long text, and an optional new photo via `PATCH /api/inventory/:id`. The slug is not regenerated. Older bios without `longBody` open with the blank-line split already filled in. **New address, handover:** the consultations follow the item, so the new address sees them on `/account` at once and the old one loses them, but the emails about them went to the old address (or nowhere). So when a save gives the mentor a different address (not just different case; clearing it sends nothing), `sendMentorEmailHandover` emails the new address each one it missed: **New consultation request** for every pending request and the expert's **Consultation scheduled** (Zoom link, `.ics` naming the new address) for every meeting not yet held, in paced rounds. Members get nothing. The response's `handover` (`{ count, emailSent }`, or null) picks Kimchi `kimchi.mentor_updated_handover` when `emailSent`, else `kimchi.mentor_updated`; the audit line adds `handedOver`.
3. Pending list shows requested date, time slots, summary, and the same **Meeting date and time** field (`MeetingTimePicker`, required `meetingAt`). No time shows `admin.meeting_time_required` and a past time `admin.meeting_time_past`, under the field, before the confirmation; the field gets focus. Approve and Refuse then ask an inline question in the row (`InlineConfirm`, the same `admin.*_confirm` text; **Keep it** or Escape returns focus to the button). On phones the expertise row stacks (it also carries `consultation-row`). **Approve = schedule** (same `runScheduleConsultation` as the expert path: Zoom + both emails). Refuse sends the member the consultation decline email. A request for a mentor with no expert email carries a **No mentor email** pill (`admin.no_mentor_email`, also on that mentor's scheduled, not-held rows in Edit Reservations), and its approve, follow-up, delete and remove confirms use the `admin.*_no_expert_email` versions: only the member is invited, so staff send the mentor the Zoom link. Kimchi says "scheduled" (`kimchi.consultation_scheduled`, `_no_expert_email`, `_no_expert_email_no_zoom` or `_plain`) and, on refuse, `kimchi.consultation_refused` (`_plain` when `emailSent === false`).
4. Edit Reservations shows the meeting time, Zoom link and member email. **Delete** of an active consultation (or deleting the expertise item) deletes the Zoom meeting and emails both as an admin cancellation; its inline question names the member (`admin.delete_consultation_confirm`). Held meetings are labeled **Completed** (`account_consultations.status_completed`, a grey sticker); deleting one only removes the record, and its question says nobody is notified (`admin.delete_held_consultation_confirm`). Follow-ups carry a **Follow-up** badge. A scheduled meeting that qualifies, from its day on (same `canBookFollowUp()` rule as `/account`, computed from the item's reservations), gets **Book a follow-up**: the same panel as the expert's (`admin.follow_up_intro`, `MeetingTimePicker`, optional note) → `admin.follow_up_confirm` → `POST /api/consultations/:id/follow-up`; for admins the response also carries the full `item`, which replaces the item's reservations like approve does. Expertise rows here carry `consultation-row`, so they stack on phones.
5. **Remove Item** on an expertise item with any consultation rows warns that its consultation records will be permanently deleted (they cascade with the item) and gives the count (`admin.remove_expertise_confirm`). Pending and upcoming consultations are still cancelled with emails; held ones are deleted without notice.
6. **Needs attention** (`AdminAttention.svelte`): its **Overdue** tile counts consultation requests waiting a week or more (`isOverdueRequest`, `CONSULTATION_WAIT_DAYS` = 7), those Pending rows carry a **Waiting a week+** chip (`admin.waiting_week`), and Kimchi's arrival note (B2) uses the same rule. **Consultations, next 7 days** counts scheduled, not-held meetings starting within a week (or under way) and opens Edit Reservations.
7. **Members** (`AdminUsers.svelte`): **Suspend** and **Delete** refuse a member who is the `expert_email` on an expertise item (409 `user_is_mentor` with that item's `title`; change or remove the mentor profile first), because a suspended or deleted mentor's profile would keep taking requests nobody could schedule. Deleting a member cancels their pending and not-yet-held consultations as an admin cancel (Zoom deleted, both emailed), then strips `user_email`, `time_slots` and `request_summary` from every row and sets `anonymized_at` (migration 009), and gives every item the address owns (`owner_email`, 015) to samuel@apathyisboring.com (`reassignItemsOwnedBy(email, CONTACT_TO)`, audit line `reassign_owned_items`) before the auth user is deleted. Their held meetings stay as history with no member: the expert's `/account` row reads **Account deleted** (`account_consultations.member_deleted`) with no **Book a follow-up**, and the follow-up route answers 400 `follow_up_not_available`.
8. **Create Reservation** (`AdminCreateReservation.svelte`) books a meeting with a mentor for any email address, member or not, when someone asks staff directly. Pick the mentor under **Expertise** in the item list: the dates give way to **Meeting date and time** (device time zone, as on Pending) and an optional **What they'd like to discuss** (≤2000, stored as `request_summary`), and the box under the picker lists the mentor's upcoming meetings and pending requests. `POST /api/admin/inventory/:id/reservations { email, meetingAt, summary?, message? }` → `addReservation` with `meetingAt` and the Zoom callbacks: inside the item lock, the one-open-request rule (409 `reservation_consultation_open` when that address already has a pending request or an upcoming meeting with this mentor; schedule or cancel that one instead), then the Zoom meeting (502 `reservation_zoom_failed`, nothing saved), then a `reserved` row dated today (Montréal) with `meeting_at`, the Zoom fields and `booked_by_staff` (the meeting is deleted again if the insert fails). Both get the scheduled emails (trigger 6 in `docs/automated-emails.md`), the member's worded for a staff booking (who booked it, the topic, and an invitation to log in or create an account with that address to see or cancel it). No Slack post. A mentor with no email carries the **No mentor email** pill and the `_no_expert_email` confirm, as on Pending. It is then a normal scheduled consultation: in Edit Reservations, on both `/account` pages, cancellable, movable by the mentor (or owner) from `/account`, and follow-ups work from its day on.
9. There is no `/admin` button to move a meeting or message a member; an admin can call `POST /api/consultations/:id/reschedule` (the route allows admins) or act from `/account` on a mentor they own.

## Zoom (`src/lib/zoom.js`)

| Concern | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| Auth | Server-to-Server OAuth (`ZOOM_ACCOUNT_ID`, `ZOOM_CLIENT_ID`, `ZOOM_CLIENT_SECRET`); token cached until ~1 min before expiry |
| Host | `ZOOM_HOST_USER` (default `me` = the app owner's licensed Zoom user). Members/experts don't need Zoom accounts |
| Meeting | Scheduled (type 2), 60 min, `America/Toronto`, `join_before_host: true`, `jbh_time: 0`, `waiting_room: false`, passcode auto-generated by Zoom. Topic `ARL consultation: {title}` (summary is **not** sent to Zoom) |
| Screen share for all | Host-user setting, not per-meeting. Attempted once per process via `PATCH /users/{host}/settings` — requires `user:update:settings:admin` scope; otherwise set **Who can share = All Participants** in the Zoom web portal |
| Create failure | Inside the item lock, before the DB write → 502, reservation stays `pending` (a follow-up saves nothing). DB error after create → meeting deleted (also for a failed follow-up insert). The update is guarded with `.eq('status', 'pending').select('id')`; if no row changed (request no longer pending), the meeting is deleted and the route returns 400 with no emails |
| Delete | On cancel / admin delete of a consultation that is not yet held; 404 treated as success; other errors logged (cancellation still succeeds) |
| Move | `POST /api/consultations/:id/reschedule` → `rescheduleConsultation` (item lock) calls `moveZoomMeetingForConsultation` (`server.js`) before the DB write: `moveConsultationMeeting(meetingId, { startTime })` sends `PATCH /meetings/{id}` with `start_time` as Montréal wall-clock time and `timezone: America/Toronto`, keeping the id, join link and passcode. A 404 (Zoom no longer has it) or no meeting id (scheduled while Zoom was off) → a new meeting is created (`createZoomMeetingForConsultation`) and its fields are saved with the new time; that meeting is deleted again if the DB write then fails or finds the row no longer scheduled. Any other Zoom error → 502 `consultation_zoom_failed`, nothing saved, no email. A meeting moved in place is not moved back if the DB write fails afterwards (unlikely: the item lock covers cancels) |
| Not configured | Schedules without a meeting; emails say a link will follow. A move changes only `meeting_at` (logged) |
| Invitations | Zoom's API does not email anyone — invitations are our Resend emails + `.ics` (`METHOD:REQUEST`, cancel sends `METHOD:CANCEL` with the same UID). `SEQUENCE` is the time the file was built in seconds since 1970 (`buildConsultationIcs`; nothing stores a revision), so a later invite (a move, a handover) or cancel always replaces an earlier one for the same UID |

- A non-staff owner of a mentor with no expert email (only possible when staff clear the expert email of a member's own profile) gets no "new request", reminder or "moved" email for its consultations: the mentor's emails go to `expert_email` only. Clearing a member's expert email keeps them as the owner, by design (the profile stays theirs to manage). The **Members** suspend and delete guards (409 `user_is_mentor`) still match `expert_email` only; a delete gives the account's owned items to samuel@apathyisboring.com.
- Owners' actions on rooms, equipment and books use the library team's wording in emails ("The Activist Resource Library team changed…", `cancelled_by: 'owner'` reads as the team), because only admins create those items today.
- Approve still refuses when another pending request overlaps the dates (409 `reservation_collision`), for owners on `/account` as for admins.
- Reminders depend on site traffic: the pass runs on incoming requests (as the GA pass does), so if nobody hits the site between 24 h and 1 h before a start, that booking gets no reminder. Not sent in local dev (deployed service only).
- Room collision still ignores hours: two bookings of a room on the same day always collide, also when an owner chooses new dates.
- Kimchi's load-time bubbles B4, B5 and B6 on `/account` also cover the consultations of mentors with no email that the account owns (today samuel@apathyisboring.com's).
- An owner's **Choose new dates** keeps a start date in the past allowed for a loan already under way (the end can't be before today).
- Server emails are English-only, so their text wordmark reads APATHY IS BORING. A French one (L'APATHIE C'EST PLATE) waits for French email copy.
- Slack workflow must accept the extra payload keys (`time_slots`, `request_summary`, `expert_email`, `admin_url`), plus `message`, and its message is just `{message}` (the server writes the wording for every event). Slack does not fire on schedule/cancel/move, messages, reminders or owners' actions (it does on a follow-up).
- Self-published mentor profiles still have no reserved-name check, no "community mentor" label, and no approval step; staff see them through the Slack alert and can remove them from **Remove Item**.
